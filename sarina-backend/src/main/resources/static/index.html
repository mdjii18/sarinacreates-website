The Journey of Your Custom Piece

Resin art takes patience and passion. Each piece undergoes days of meticulous layering and curing.

01

Share Your Story

Tell us your occasion, preferred colors, or courier your wedding flowers / keepsake photos directly to Sarina's studio.

02

Floral & Color Prep

Real flowers are preserved with specialized silica drying techniques. Pigments and metallic flakes are hand-mixed for rich color harmony.

03

Multi-Layer Pour

Poured in multiple micro-layers over 5 to 7 days to eliminate air bubbles and create profound optical glass depth.

04

Hand-Polished & Gifted

Every piece is wet-sanded, diamond polished, and safely gift-packaged ready to adorn your home or be gifted.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I courier my wedding varmala or flowers?

Once you connect on WhatsApp, we provide complete, easy packing guidelines to courier fresh or semi-dried florals safely to our studio from anywhere in India.

How long does a custom resin keepsake take?

Custom crafting typically takes 10 to 14 working days (4–5 days for delicate floral drying, followed by multi-layered pouring and 7 full days of curing for diamond clarity). The overall delivery timeline may vary depending on your delivery location and how large or detailed your order is. Sarina will provide an exact, personalized delivery date when discussing your custom piece on WhatsApp!

Will the flowers or photos fade over time?

We use premium UV-resistant, non-yellowing artist-grade epoxy resin. Keepsakes stay crystal clear for decades when kept away from direct, harsh sun and high heat.

Can I request custom colors or themes?

Absolutely! Sarina custom-matches any color theme — whether you desire soft blush pink, ocean turquoise, royal burgundy, or pure white with 24k gold leaf foil.
